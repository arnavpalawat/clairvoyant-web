# Brand Voice Guidelines

## Tone
- **Confident but not arrogant** — we know we're good, but we're not bragging
- **Clear and direct** — no jargon, no fluff
- **Slightly playful** — we're serious about the product, not about ourselves
- **Empathetic** — we understand the pain of information overload

## Words We Use
- Anticipate, proactive, context, surface, insight
- Clarity, focus, prepared, ahead
- Simple, smart, seamless

## Words We Avoid
- Revolutionary, disruptive, game-changing (overused)
- AI-powered (implied, don't need to say it constantly)
- Synergy, leverage, optimize (corporate speak)
- Just, simply, easily (minimizing words)

## Example Transformations

❌ "Our AI-powered platform leverages machine learning to optimize your workflow."

✅ "Clairvoyant watches your calendar and email, then tells you what matters."

---

❌ "Simply connect your accounts to get started on your productivity journey."

✅ "Connect your accounts. That's it."

---

❌ "Revolutionary technology that will transform how you work."

✅ "The right context at the right time. Finally."
